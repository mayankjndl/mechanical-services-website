Mechanical Maintenance

Comprehensive mechanical support covering the full lifecycle of your industrial machinery — from emergency breakdown to routine preventive care.

🔥 Breakdown Maintenance

On-call emergency response when a machine fails unexpectedly. We diagnose the root cause, rectify the fault, and verify full restoration — minimising production downtime at every step.

📅 Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled, systematic inspection and servicing at agreed intervals. Covers lubrication, tightening, cleaning, wear checks, and early-stage fault detection before they escalate into breakdowns.

🏗️ Installation & Commissioning

Mechanical installation of new or relocated industrial machinery including structural mounting, guarding, pipe work connections, and functional commissioning runs to confirm correct operation.

📐 Alignment & Leveling

Precision shaft alignment using appropriate methods, machine leveling on foundations or vibration mounts, and geometric checks on machine tables and guides to restore accuracy.

🔩 Bearing, Belt & Gearbox Replacement

Diagnosis and replacement of worn rotating components including bearings, V-belts, timing belts, couplings, and gearbox units — using correct specification parts for lasting repair.

🛠️ Hydraulic & Pneumatic System Work

Fault diagnosis and repair on hydraulic power packs, cylinders, valves, and pneumatic circuits — including seal replacement, pressure adjustment, and leak rectification.

🔍 System Troubleshooting

Methodical fault-finding on complex mechanical systems — identifying intermittent faults, vibration causes, overheating sources, and accuracy deviations on precision machines.

ElectricalMaintenance

Electrical Maintenance

Safe, professional electrical maintenance for industrial machinery — covering motors, control systems, wiring, and instrumentation.

🔌 Motor Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Inspection, insulation testing, and servicing of three-phase and single-phase electric motors. Bearing replacement, winding checks, and operational testing to confirm correct output.

🎛️ Control Panel Inspection & Repair

Systematic inspection and repair of industrial electrical panels — including contactors, relays, circuit breakers, fuses, terminal blocks, and basic instrumentation.

📡 Sensors & Limit Switch Servicing

Testing, adjustment, and replacement of proximity sensors, photoelectric sensors, limit switches, and encoders — essential for machine cycle accuracy and safety interlocking.

🔧 Wiring, Cabling & Terminations

Cable laying, correct termination, and rewiring for machine installations, panel modifications, and fault rectification — all carried out safely and to standard.

⚡ Electrical Breakdown Response

Rapid on-call response for electrical faults causing machine trips, failure to start, nuisance tripping, or erratic operation — minimising production disruption.

Breakdown vs Preventive Maintenance

Both services play a role in keeping your machinery operational. Understanding the difference helps you plan the right maintenance strategy for your plant.

⚡ Breakdown Maintenance

Reactive response when a machine has already failed and production is affected.

  • Called in when a machine stops or faults
  • Priority is restoring operation as quickly as possible
  • Root cause identified and rectified on-site
  • Suitable for unexpected, unplanned failures
  • Available on-call for urgent situations
  • Parts sourced and fitted during same visit where possible
  • Post-repair run test to confirm full restoration

🛡️ Preventive Maintenance

Proactive, scheduled servicing to avoid failures before they occur.

  • Carried out at agreed intervals (weekly, monthly, quarterly)
  • Identifies and addresses wear before machines fail
  • Reduces unexpected breakdowns significantly
  • Covers lubrication, tightening, wear checks, cleaning
  • Extends machine service life and component longevity
  • Planned around your production schedule
  • Supports consistent machine accuracy and output quality

Most manufacturing facilities benefit from a combination of both — a preventive programme as the primary strategy, supported by our on-call breakdown team when the unexpected happens.

🖥️ CNC / VMC / HMC Operators

Experienced machine operators capable of running CNC Turning, VMC Milling, and HMC machining centres — including program loading, tool setting, and basic offset adjustments on common controller platforms.

🔧 Mechanical & Electrical Technicians

Diploma or ITI-qualified technicians with practical experience in industrial maintenance tasks — covering mechanical systems, electrical panels, and machine servicing across various manufacturing environments.

🔩 Fitters

Skilled in precision fitting, assembly, and mechanical component installation. Experience with jigs, fixtures, and mechanical assembly on production lines and maintenance applications.

Reliable general production operators and helpers for assembly, packing, material movement, and support tasks — deployed quickly to meet changing production headcount needs.

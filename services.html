MechanicalWhat We Do

Mechanical Maintenance Services

Full-range mechanical support covering breakdown recovery, scheduled preventive work, component replacement, and machine commissioning.

🔥 Breakdown Maintenance

Emergency on-call response when a machine fails unexpectedly. Our technicians diagnose the fault, source replacement parts if required, and restore the machine to full working order as quickly as possible.

📅 Preventive Maintenance

Scheduled inspection and servicing visits carried out at agreed intervals. Covers lubrication, tightening, wear checks, and early-stage fault identification before they cause production loss.

🏗️ Installation & Commissioning

Mechanical installation of new or relocated industrial machinery, including structural mounting, piping connections, guarding, and functional commissioning runs.

📐 Alignment & Leveling

Precision shaft alignment, machine leveling, and geometric accuracy checks. Correct alignment extends bearing life, reduces vibration, and improves machine output quality.

🔩 Bearing, Belt & Gearbox Replacement

Diagnosis and replacement of worn bearings, drive belts, gearboxes, and related drive components — using correct specification parts to ensure reliable operation.

🛠️ System Troubleshooting

Methodical fault-finding on complex mechanical systems including hydraulic circuits, pneumatic systems, and multi-axis machine mechanisms.

ElectricalWhat We Do

Electrical Maintenance Services

Comprehensive electrical support for industrial machinery — ensuring safe, compliant, and efficient electrical systems on your plant floor.

🔌 Motor Maintenance & Troubleshooting

Inspection, testing, and servicing of industrial electric motors — including winding checks, insulation testing, bearing replacement, and rewinding where required.

🎛️ Control Panel Inspection & Repair

Inspection, fault diagnosis, and repair of electrical control panels — including contactors, relays, fuses, MCBs, and PLC I/O points where accessible.

📡 Sensors & Limit Switch Servicing

Testing and replacement of proximity sensors, limit switches, encoders, and position detectors — critical for machine cycle accuracy and safety interlocking.

🔧 Wiring & Cabling Work

Cable laying, termination, and rewiring for machine installations, panel upgrades, and fault rectification — carried out safely and to standard.

⚡ Electrical Breakdown Response

On-call electrical fault diagnosis and repair when machines trip, fail to start, or display electrical faults — minimising downtime and production loss.
